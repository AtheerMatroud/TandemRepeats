\section{Session: Y-chromosome data acquisition (24 Aug 2026)}

\subsection{Objective}
Identify and obtain long-read, fully assembled human Y-chromosome sequences
suitable for tandem-repeat analysis (e.g.\ DYZ1/DYZ2 heterochromatin blocks,
the DYZ3 centromeric $\alpha$-satellite array, and the TSPY repeat array),
which short-read datasets such as the original 1000~Genomes releases cannot
resolve.

\subsection{Dataset selected}
\textbf{Hallast, Ebert, Loftus et al.\ (2023)}, ``Assembly of 43 human
Y~chromosomes reveals extensive complexity and variation'',
\emph{Nature} \textbf{621}, 344--354.
\href{https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06425-6}{doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06425-6}.

The dataset comprises 43 male samples spanning approximately 183{,}000 years
of Y-chromosome divergence, assembled by the Human Genome Structural
Variation Consortium (HGSVC) from PacBio HiFi and Oxford Nanopore ultralong
reads. Most samples have contiguous assemblies of the repeat regions of
interest.

\subsection{Download locations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Primary FTP mirror (assembled genomes and related files):
  \begin{lstlisting}
https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/working/20230412_sigY_assembly_data/
  \end{lstlisting}
  \item Archived INSDC records: BioProjects \texttt{PRJEB58376} and
  \texttt{PRJNA988114} (assemblies); underlying sequencing reads under
  \texttt{PRJEB41077}, \texttt{PRJEB39684}, \texttt{PRJEB39750}.
  \item Reference-grade complete Y for comparison: T2T-CHM13v2.0
  (includes the HG002 T2T-Y), NCBI assembly \texttt{GCA\_009914755.4}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scripts produced}
Two equivalent download scripts were written and placed in the project
folder (both mirror the FTP directory and are safe to re-run; complete
files are skipped):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{download\_hallast\_y43.sh} --- Bash/wget version, for
  WSL, Git~Bash, Linux or macOS:
  \begin{lstlisting}
bash download_hallast_y43.sh [output_dir]
  \end{lstlisting}
  \item \texttt{download\_hallast\_y43.py} --- pure-Python (standard
  library only) version that runs natively on Windows:
  \begin{lstlisting}
python download_hallast_y43.py [output_dir]
  \end{lstlisting}
\end{itemize}
Default output directory: \texttt{hallast\_y43\_assemblies/}.
